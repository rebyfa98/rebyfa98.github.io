My research focuses on developing methods for reliable uncertainty quantification in complex data settings. I am particularly interested in applications to survival analysis and biostatistics, where outcomes are often only partially observed and where uncertainty quantification is essential for scientific decision-making.  I am also broadly interested in causal inference, nonparametric and semiparametric inference, survival analysis, biostatistics, and Bayesian methodology.
